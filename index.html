How To Set Up
  1. 1 Fill in your Name, Position, and Mobile (logo and email are optional), then click Generate My Signature.
  2. 2 Click Copy Signature — this copies the fully formatted signature to your clipboard.
  3. 3 Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Paste
    Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Signature → Paste
Note: the logo is embedded directly in the signature (no external hosting needed). Gmail and Apple Mail render this reliably. Desktop Outlook occasionally strips embedded images on paste — if that happens, re-save the logo as a compressed PNG under 100KB and try again.
